# Bulgarian genealogical collections: practical routes

Last reviewed for institutional existence and published descriptions: 2026-11-17. Verify current catalogue and service status at the time of research.

## State Archives Agency (Държавна агенция „Архиви“)
https://archives.government.bg/

The official agency portal is the starting point for central and regional state archives, archival services, finding aids and digitised material. Select the archive by historical jurisdiction and record creator, not merely today's nearest city. Check whether a catalogue description, digital image or original consultation is actually available.

When searching, use both current and historical forms of a settlement, district and institution. Record the fonds (фонд), inventory (опис), file (а.е.), sheet (лист) and document date exactly as supplied by the archive.

## Central State Archives and Bulgarian Exarchate material

The Central State Archives may hold records of central religious, administrative and institutional bodies. A published study of the Bulgarian Exarchate census/registration of Bulgarians in Thessaloniki in 1907 reports household-level and individual information for a substantial part of the Exarchate-registered community.

Academic publication:
https://www.solunbg.org/images/2020/Izvestia_na_Darjavnite_Arhivi-T-115-16-2018-c330-509.pdf

The article reports approximately 841 households and 3,275 persons in the material it analyses. It describes data such as sex, age, occupation/social status, literacy and household relationships. This is not a complete census of every Bulgarian-speaking or Bulgarian-identifying person in Thessaloniki: other confessional/community groups are outside its scope. It does not systematically provide all birth dates or places of origin.

Important: household membership and a recorded household relationship must be transcribed exactly; do not reinterpret household grouping as biological kinship without explicit evidence. The article is a scholarly analysis, not a substitute for the archival item and its exact reference.

## Cross-border records: Varna and Greek institutional catalogues

IMS (Institute for Mediterranean Studies) lists Varna parish registers for 1887–1905 among its databases:
https://ims.forth.gr/en/page/view?id=56

This is a useful lead for a Greek-community or cross-border research path. Before using it as evidence, determine the parish, denomination, original record creator, current custodian, digitisation source and whether the database is an index or image/transcription. Do not assume that a database hosted by an institute is the archive of origin.

## Research sequence

1. Establish the historical administrative and ecclesiastical jurisdiction for the event date.
2. Search the official archive portal and identify the likely regional or central archive.
3. Search in Bulgarian Cyrillic, older spellings and alternate settlement names.
4. Ask the archive about surviving parish, civil, municipal, school, military, court, property and refugee series as relevant.
5. Capture exact fonds/inventory/file/folio references and request the full image where possible.
6. Cross-check with neighbouring communities and migration destinations.

## Limitations

- Online portals and digitisation change; a listed archive does not imply online images.
- Parish registers depend on denomination and local survival.
- A record of a person in an Exarchate register documents registration in that institutional context, not a complete statement of modern ethnicity or citizenship.
- Absence from a particular religious register does not prove absence from the locality.
