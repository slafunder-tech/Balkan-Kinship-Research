# Greek genealogical collections: practical routes

Last reviewed for existence and published collection descriptions: 2026-11-17. This is a discovery guide, not proof that a particular person appears in a collection. Confirm current access, coverage and image availability before ordering or citing a record.

## Start with jurisdiction, not nationality

Identify the historical municipality, community, parish/metropolis and date. Greece's territorial expansion means civil and church record start dates vary by region. A record for a place now in Greece may be held locally, by a General State Archives (GAK / Γενικά Αρχεία του Κράτους) service, municipality or ecclesiastical authority. Do not assume one national online register covers all periods.

## Useful collections and portals

### Greek Ancestry — collections
https://greekancestry.net/collections/

Published collection descriptions include:
- Voter lists, roughly 1840s–1930s, with coverage varying by area and series. Entries may include name, father's name, age, occupation, property or residence.
- Asia Minor refugee registers, described as roughly 540,000 indexed entries for refugees who moved from Turkey to Greece after the 1923 population exchange; may include origin place and father/spouse.
- Business directories, 1901–1947, useful for occupation and address leads.
- Male registers (`Μητρώα Αρρένων`) for selected areas and periods; may identify father and birth details.

These are indexed collections from a private genealogy platform, not a complete national civil register. Record the exact collection, locality and original record reference. Search results are leads until the underlying record is examined.

### Greek Family History (Newcastle project)
https://apps.ncl.ac.uk/GreekFamilyHistory/Page/About
https://apps.ncl.ac.uk/GreekFamilyHistory/Page/Links

Useful orientation to:
- municipal/family registers (`Δημοτολόγιο`);
- male registers (`Μητρώα Αρρένων`);
- civil registration;
- church registers and local archive holdings;
- regional variation in record start dates and custody.

Use it to identify the likely record creator and custodian. The site's examples are not universal coverage statements.

### Institute for Mediterranean Studies / IMS (ΙΜΣ)
https://ims.forth.gr/en/page/view?id=56

Published databases include selected municipal rolls, censuses, electoral registers, tax registers and refugee-related material. Examples listed by the institute include Hermoupolis, Piraeus, Corfu, Kea and Rethymno collections, and a municipal roll of Asia Minor refugees in Syros. The portal also describes Varna parish registers (1887–1905); investigate the exact record provenance and custody rather than assuming the institute is the original archive.

### Foundation of the Hellenic World — Genealogy and Oral History
https://www.ime.gr/depts/genealogy/index.php?lang_id=en&sid=1&view=page

Potentially useful for family histories, oral testimony and materials concerning refugees from Asia Minor, the Black Sea, Thrace and Constantinople. Treat oral histories as attributed testimony, not automatically contemporary documentary proof. Respect privacy and access restrictions.

## Research sequence

1. Establish the historical locality and its date-specific jurisdiction.
2. Search name variants in Greek script and likely local orthographies.
3. Identify the municipality and parish/metropolis; ask what register series survive and where they are held.
4. Search male registers for a male ancestor, but do not neglect municipal, civil, church and refugee records for women and whole families.
5. Follow marriage records for maiden names, parents and witnesses.
6. For post-1923 refugee families, search origin-place registers and both sides of the Aegean migration.
7. Capture full archival citation, page/image and exact original wording.

## Limitations

- A collection's advertised date range does not mean every locality or year survives.
- Index coverage is not the same as image access or full transcription.
- Male registers are male-focused and cannot serve as a complete family tree.
- A shared surname, village or refugee origin is not proof of kinship.
