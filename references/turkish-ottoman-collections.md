# Turkish and Ottoman genealogical collections

Last reviewed for institutional descriptions: 2026-11-17. Separate modern Turkish civil-registration records from Ottoman-era archival records; they have different creators, access rules and evidentiary meanings.

## Modern Turkish civil-registration archive — Nüfus ve Vatandaşlık İşleri (NVI)
https://www.nvi.gov.tr/sss-arsiv-hizmetleri

The official FAQ describes archival series including:
- `Atik Nüfus Kütükleri` from 1883 (1299);
- `Sicil Vukuat Defterleri`;
- general family registers from 1904 (1320);
- local/residence-related registers from 1925–1926 onward;
- event registers (`Vukuat Defterleri`) for births, marriages, divorces, moves, corrections, re-registration and deaths, with a stated range beginning in 1904 and continuing into the modern period.

Access is subject to legal relationship and identity verification. The official FAQ distinguishes information that may be requested about oneself, spouse, ancestors and descendants from restricted collateral relatives. It describes provision of written information under conditions, not unrestricted delivery of original archival books. Recheck the current rules before applying.

These are modern state population registers. They are not equivalent to Ottoman `nüfus defterleri`, parish books or tax registers.

## Turkish e-Government — Alt-Üst Soy Bilgisi
https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/nvi-alt-ust-soy-bilgisi-sorgulama

Official online service requiring authenticated access. Do not promise access to a person without the required Turkish e-Devlet credentials and eligibility. Treat downloaded lineage information as a lead and seek the underlying registration evidence where possible.

## Presidency State Archives / Devlet Arşivleri
Research rules:

Online catalogue:
https://katalog.devletarsivleri.gov.tr/

The official research guidance describes catalogue searching and access to catalogue descriptions and selected preliminary images; some document images can be ordered, and reading-room research is available under current procedures. Catalogue metadata is not the same as a full document image. Record the exact archive code and document identifier.

The 2017 `Osmanlı Arşivi Rehberi` is useful historical orientation to the archive and series, but may not reflect current interfaces or fees:

## Turkish Historical Society archive — Türk Tarih Kurumu (TTK)
https://ttk.gov.tr/en/arsiv/

A further catalogue route for documents, photographs, periodicals and press clippings relating to late Ottoman and Republican history. Language varies; many Ottoman-era materials are in Ottoman Turkish, while other languages also occur. Verify item-level description and access.

## Ottoman record families: do not conflate

Search for the relevant series and record purpose; names and dates may be recorded differently.

- `Nüfus defterleri`: population registers, often organized by locality and population category; coverage and fields vary by date and series.
- `Tahrir defterleri`: fiscal/land and population-related surveys, with a different administrative purpose from modern civil registration.
- `Temettuat defterleri`: tax/income/property-related registers, useful for household economy and residence, not civil birth certificates.
- `Şer'iyye sicilleri`: Islamic court registers; may contain inheritance, marriage, guardianship, property and disputes, depending on court and survival.
- `Salname`: yearbooks, often administrative/institutional context, not necessarily person-level genealogy.
- `Vakıf` records: endowments and associated property/administration; useful selectively.

The word `hane` can denote an administrative, fiscal or household unit depending on the record. It does not by itself establish a nuclear family or biological relationship. Likewise, `nefer`, status labels and religious/community categories must be read in the specific series and period.

| Osmanlı Arşivi | Ottoman Archives |
| şer'iyye sicili | Islamic court register |
| tahrir defteri | fiscal survey |
| temettuat defteri | tax/income register |
| muhacir / muhacirin | migrant/refugee, context-specific |
| iskân | settlement/resettlement |
| hane | household/unit, context-dependent |

Ottoman Turkish is written in an Arabic-derived script with historical orthography. Do not assume modern Turkish spellings can be mechanically converted into Ottoman script. Search catalogue descriptions using modern Turkish, Ottoman terms, historical place names and known administrative units; obtain specialist palaeographic help where needed.

## Research sequence

1. Determine whether the target event falls under modern Turkish civil registration or Ottoman administration.
2. For modern records, verify eligibility and relationship documentation with NVI.
3. For Ottoman-era research, search Devlet Arşivleri catalogue by locality, administrative unit, record series and institution.
4. Record archive code, catalogue description, document image status and order/access conditions separately.
5. Interpret each series by its administrative purpose; never extract civil relationships from a tax/household grouping without explicit support.
6. Search parallel records in successor states and destination countries after migration or border changes.
